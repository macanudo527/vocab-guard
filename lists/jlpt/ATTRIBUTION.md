# Attribution

The files in this directory are generated by `scripts/fetch_lists.sh` from:

- **Vocabulary:** [open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks)
  by Jamie Sinclair (MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 Jamie Sinclair).
- **Kanji levels:** [JLPT_Vocabulary](https://github.com/Bluskyo/JLPT_Vocabulary) by Bluskyo (MIT).

Both are based on Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources, <http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/>,
licensed under Creative Commons "BY". Credit: **Jonathan Waller, tanos.co.uk**.

The data was changed: it was reformatted into `word,reading,level` and `kanji,level`,
spelling variants were split into separate rows, notes and affix marks were removed,
readings were converted to hiragana, and duplicates were kept at the easiest level.
Details, pinned commits and the license evidence are in [../SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).

These lists are community lists. The JLPT has published no official vocabulary or
kanji lists for N5–N1.
